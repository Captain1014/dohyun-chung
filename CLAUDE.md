## Project

**Google Cloud Customer Engineer Preparation**

A comprehensive study and preparation program for Dohyun Chung to transition from AWS/SaaS background to Google Cloud Customer Engineer at Google Korea. Covers GCP technical knowledge, Associate Cloud Engineer certification (June 2026 exam), Google Cloud Next '25 announcements, and CE-specific interview preparation.

**Core Value:** Master Google Cloud services deeply enough to confidently demonstrate technical expertise and customer-facing solution design in the CE interview process.

### Constraints

- **Timeline**: ACE exam in June 2026 (~3 months), CE application in ~6 months
- **Learning Style**: Explanation + Quiz verification + Documentation of all studied content
- **Language**: Study materials in English, communication in Korean
- **Focus**: Google Cloud ecosystem only — no multi-cloud distraction

## Technology Stack

## Recommended Study Stack
### Primary Learning Platform
| Resource | Cost | Purpose | Why |
|----------|------|---------|-----|
| Google Cloud Skills Boost | $29/month or free credits | Official labs (Qwiklabs) + courses | The only hands-on platform with real GCP sandboxes; aligned to ACE exam; Skills Badges are verifiable credentials |
| Udemy ACE Course | ~$15-20 (sale price) | Video lecture foundation | Highest-enrollment ACE course (400k+ students), demo-heavy, regularly updated for 2025 exam changes |
### Practice Exam Platform
| Resource | Cost | Purpose | Why |
|----------|------|---------|-----|
| ExamTopics ACE | Free (186 Qs) | Real exam question bank | Community-reported actual exam questions with discussion threads that explain reasoning |
| Udemy Practice Exams (1500 Qs) | ~$15 | High-volume drilling | Volume matters in final sprint; 1500 questions covers all topic combinations |
| Whizlabs ACE | Free (50 Qs) / Paid | Secondary practice set | Strong explanation quality; good for understanding WHY not just WHAT |
### Reference Documentation
| Resource | Cost | Purpose | Why |
|----------|------|---------|-----|
| Google Cloud Documentation | Free | Authoritative source of truth | When ExamTopics has disputed answers, official docs win |
| ACE Exam Guide PDF | Free | Topic checklist | Use as study progress tracker; every bullet point is a potential exam question |
### CE Interview Preparation
| Resource | Cost | Purpose | Why |
|----------|------|---------|-----|
| Vertex AI documentation | Free | AI/ML depth for CE | CE interviews in 2026 center on AI use cases; must be fluent in Vertex AI ecosystem |
| Google Cloud Next '25 blog | Free | Current GCP direction | CE role requires knowing what Google is selling TODAY, not just what exists |
| Glassdoor CE interview reports | Free | Interview structure intel | Best available community data on CE interview rounds and question types |
### Hands-On Environment
| Resource | Cost | Purpose | Why |
|----------|------|---------|-----|
| GCP Free Tier + $300 trial credit | Free | Personal GCP sandbox | Projects 1-4 can run within free tier limits if resources are cleaned up promptly |
| Cloud Shell | Free | In-browser terminal | Zero setup; available in any GCP project; use for gcloud + kubectl practice |
## Alternatives Considered
| Category | Recommended | Alternative | Why Not |
|----------|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Video course | Udemy (400k students, ~$15) | Pluralsight ($45/month) | Udemy ACE course is community-proven and cheaper; Pluralsight quality is good but expensive for single cert prep |
| Hands-on labs | Cloud Skills Boost (official) | Linux Foundation / A Cloud Guru | Skills Boost uses real GCP projects (not simulated environments) and earns official Google skill badges |
| Practice exams | ExamTopics + Udemy 1500 Qs | TutorialsDojo | TutorialsDojo is excellent for AWS; GCP coverage is thinner; ExamTopics has more GCP community volume |
| Official study book | N/A | Wiley "Official GCP ACE Study Guide 2nd Ed" | Book-based study is slower than interactive labs; use only if video + labs are insufficient |
| Note-taking | Personal documentation system | Anki flashcards | Project already uses quiz-based learning per CLAUDE.md preference; integrate into existing workflow |
