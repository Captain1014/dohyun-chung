### Q1. Tell me about a time you resolved a difficult technical issue for a customer.

**🇺🇸 English**

So one weekend I got this urgent message — one of our POS users is saying they can't create payroll, the merchant is upset, and our support team is kind of freaking out.

I went in right away and looked at everything. And the system was completely fine — what happened was another cashier had already created that week's payroll, and the UI was showing a last week saved date filter, so the merchant wasn't seeing today's records. **🇺🇸 English**

## 4. Workato Knowledge (Recruiter Q&A)

### Q. "How would you describe what Workato does?"

> "workato connects basically all the tools a company uses and automates the work between them. Like if a deal closes in Salesforce, you don't want someone manually going into Jira to create a task — Workato just does it. And right now they're pushing into AI agents, where it's not just following rules anymore, the system can actually reason through what needs to happen. I've actually been building something like that in claude code in my current team so I've seen how different it feels when it works. as intended"

---

### Q. "What's a Recipe?"

> "A Recipe is the core automation unit in Workato — it starts with a trigger, something that kicks things off, and then one or more actions follow from that. I've built a few myself at my current job, connecting Google Sheets and Jira, so I have a pretty good feel for how it works in practice."

---

### Q. "How is Workato different from Zapier or MuleSoft?"

> "Zapier works until it doesn't — the moment you need real logic or scale it falls apart. MuleSoft can handle anything but you need engineers just to maintain it. Workato is the one where business teams can actually own their automations without needing dev help every time. That was what stood out to me when I used it."

---

### Q. "What's the latest direction Workato is heading?"

> "AI agents and orchestration — moving beyond rule-based flows to systems that can actually think. Agent Studio, MCP support. I've been spending time in that space personally so it's something I'm genuinely excited about."

---

### Q. "What is MCP?"

> "It's what lets AI agents connect to enterprise systems in a structured, secure way. Without it you either can't let agents touch real data, or you're building custom connections everywhere. It's what makes agents actually usable in a real company environment."

---

### Q. "Have you used MCP at your current job?"

> "Yes, actually — I use Claude Code every day at work, and it connects directly to our tools through MCP. It reads Figma designs, writes Jira tickets and comments, creates merge requests on GitLab, reads Google Sheets to track updates. So I'm not just familiar with MCP conceptually — I'm using it in a real workflow daily. It's what makes the AI actually useful in context, rather than just being a chat window."

---

### Q. "What is an AI Agent?"

> "Instead of following a fixed script, it can read a situation and decide what to do. An agent can handle things you didn't explicitly program for."

---

### Q. "What is iPaaS?"

> "Integration Platform as a Service — infrastructure that makes enterprise tools talk to each other. Workato's the market leader."
